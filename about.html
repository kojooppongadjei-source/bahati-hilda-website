About Bahati Hilda Sabiti

The clarity I teach

Certified Life & Relationship Coach · Couple Therapist · Author · Speaker

The Awakening

From heartbreak to a movement

Bahati Hilda Sabiti is a Certified Relationship, Marriage & Leadership Expert who has spent years helping leaders, lovers and visionaries communicate, build, connect and execute with clarity.

Her own journey through heartbreak and healing shaped the two books she has since written — Deliberately Selfish and Tears on My Pillow — and became the foundation for the coaching, therapy and mentorship work she now leads.

Today, that work has grown into the Accelerated Mentorship Academy, a structured programme that has graduated multiple cohorts of "Trailblazers" — professionals equipped to lead, communicate and love with more clarity than they arrived with.

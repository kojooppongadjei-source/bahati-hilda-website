About Dr. Bahati Hilda Sabiti

The clarity I teach

Global Speaker · Executive Trainer · Mentor · Broadcaster

From heartbreak to a movement

Dr. Bahati Hilda Sabiti is a global speaker, executive trainer, couple therapist and mentor, passionate about equipping individuals and organizations to thrive in life, leadership and business.

With over a decade of experience leading teams and empowering entrepreneurs, she serves as Executive Trainer at Beyond Success by John Maxwell, and as Team Leader and HR Manager at Spirit Media Group, home to Spirit TV, Spirit FM, Spirit Events and Spirit Media Hub, where she hosts The Bahati Show.

Her own journey through heartbreak and healing shaped the two books she has since written, Deliberately Selfish and Tears on My Pillow, and became the foundation for the coaching, therapy and mentorship work she now leads through the Accelerated Mentorship Academy, which has graduated multiple cohorts of "Trailblazers" equipped to lead, communicate and love with more clarity than they arrived with.

Her philanthropic initiatives support widows, orphans and women recovering from gender-based violence, helping them rebuild their lives with dignity and sustainability.
